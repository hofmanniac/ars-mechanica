<header class="page-header">
    <h1>About Ars Mechanica</h1>
    <p><strong>Ars Mechanica</strong> (Latin: "The Art of Mechanics") is a comprehensive encyclopedia of game mechanics for tabletop games, board games, RPGs, and card games. Our mission is to catalog, explain, and explore the building blocks that make games engaging, strategic, and fun.</p>
</header>

## What You'll Find Here

Each mechanic entry in Ars Mechanica includes:

- **Clear Explanations**: How the mechanic works in plain language
- **Real-World Examples**: Games that use this mechanic effectively
- **Strategic Analysis**: What makes the mechanic interesting and how it creates depth
- **Design Considerations**: Tips for implementing the mechanic in your own games
- **Common Pitfalls**: What to watch out for when using this mechanic
- **Combinations**: How the mechanic works with other mechanics to create hybrid systems

## Who Is This For?

### Game Designers
Whether you're designing your first game or your fiftieth, Ars Mechanica provides a reference library of proven mechanics and design patterns. Use it for inspiration, to understand how mechanics interact, or to find alternatives to common design challenges.

### Game Masters & Homebrewers
Modifying existing games or creating custom content? Understanding the mechanics helps you make informed changes that maintain game balance and fun.

### Players & Enthusiasts
Curious about why games work the way they do? Ars Mechanica explains the systems behind your favorite games and helps you appreciate the craft of game design.

### Students & Educators
Learning or teaching game design? These entries serve as educational resources with clear examples and analysis.

## Philosophy

### Descriptive, Not Prescriptive
We describe how mechanics work and what effects they create, but we don't claim there's only one "right" way to design games. Every mechanic has strengths and weaknesses depending on context.

### Examples Over Theory
We ground our explanations in real games whenever possible. Seeing mechanics in action helps understanding.

### Combinations Matter
Few games use mechanics in isolation. We explore how mechanics combine and interact to create emergent gameplay.

### Accessibility
We strive to make game design concepts accessible to everyone, using clear language and avoiding unnecessary jargon.

## Categories & Organization

Mechanics are organized into several top-level categories:

- **Resource Management**: Allocation, tracking, and optimization of game resources
- **Action Selection**: How players choose and execute actions
- **Turn Structure**: How turns, rounds, and phases are organized
- **Player Interaction**: Direct and indirect ways players affect each other
- **Randomness & Uncertainty**: Dice, cards, and hidden information
- **Scoring & Victory**: How players accumulate points and win
- **Character Development**: Growth, advancement, and customization
- **Spatial Mechanics**: Position, movement, and area control
- **Economic**: Markets, trading, and value exchange
- **Narrative**: Storytelling and emergent narrative systems

Many mechanics belong to multiple categories and can be discovered through tags and cross-references.

## Mechanic Combinations

One of Ars Mechanica's unique features is documenting **mechanic combinations** — how two or more mechanics work together to create hybrid systems. For example:

- **Action Points + Hand Management** = Action Programming
- **Worker Placement + Deck Building** = Deck-Building Worker Placement
- **Dice Manipulation + Worker Placement** = Dice Placement

These combinations often create entirely new strategic spaces and are worth exploring in their own right.

## Contributing

## About the Name

**Ars Mechanica** is Latin for "The Art of Mechanics." The name evokes both the systematic study of how things work (mechanica) and the creative craft (ars) of designing engaging systems.
